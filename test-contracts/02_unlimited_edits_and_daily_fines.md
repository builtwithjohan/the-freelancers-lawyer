### SECTION 1. SCOPE AND REVISIONS
Designer agrees to provide brand identity design, UI layout systems, and custom frontend integration. Client may request unlimited revisions, structural changes, redesigns, and feature additions without additional compensation until total and absolute written satisfaction is granted by Client.

### SECTION 2. DELIVERABLE DEADLINES & TIME OF THE ESSENCE
Deadlines set forth in Exhibit B are strict, time is of the essence, and punctual completion is a mandatory condition of payment.

### SECTION 3. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES & DELAY PENALTIES
If Designer fails to submit any deliverable by the exact hour and date specified in Exhibit B—regardless of whether delay was caused by Client feedback turnaround times, late asset delivery, or scope alterations—Designer shall pay Client liquidated damages of $1,000 per calendar day of delay. Client reserves the right to deduct all accumulated liquidated damages directly from outstanding invoice balances.

### SECTION 4. UNILATERAL SCOPE EXPANSION
Client may modify, expand, or adjust project scope at any time during execution. Designer agrees to incorporate all scope changes within the original fixed contract price without requesting additional fees or timeline extensions.
