# NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION COVENANT

---

### SECTION A. RESTRICTIVE NON-COMPETE
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, Contractor agrees not to perform any services, directly or indirectly, for any entity operating in the same industry or business sector as Client anywhere worldwide. Contractor shall not build, design, consult on, or engineer any competing product or service.

### SECTION B. GLOBAL NON-SOLICITATION & CLIENT LOCKOUT
Contractor shall not solicit, contract with, or perform work for any client, vendor, partner, investor, or contact of Client, or any entity introduced to Contractor during the course of the engagement.

### SECTION C. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES FOR VIOLATION
In the event Contractor breaches Section A or Section B, Contractor agrees to pay Client as liquidated damages an amount equal to five times (5x) the total fees paid to Contractor under this Agreement, plus Client's full legal expenses.
