# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

---

### 1. SERVICES & CHANGE ORDERS
Contractor agrees to perform the services detailed in Statement of Work #1. Any changes in scope, additional deliverables, or structural revisions requested by Client shall be documented in a written Change Order and billed at Contractor's standard hourly rate of $125/hr.

### 2. PAYMENT & LATE FEES
Client shall pay Contractor within fifteen (15) calendar days of invoice date. Invoices not paid within 15 days shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month. Work may be paused if invoices remain unpaid past 30 days.

### 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & OWNERSHIP
Upon receipt of full payment for services rendered, Contractor hereby grants and transfers to Client all final customized design assets and code specific to Client's brand. Contractor retains ownership of all pre-existing tools, code libraries, frameworks, and portfolio display rights.

### 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Contractor's total cumulative liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees actually paid by Client to Contractor during the three (3) months preceding the claim.

### 5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days written notice. In the event of early termination by Client, Client agrees to pay Contractor for all work completed up to the effective date of termination, plus a kill fee of 25% of the remaining contract value.
